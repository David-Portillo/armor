# Charles Stanley (1932–2023)

Senior pastor of First Baptist Church of Atlanta from the early 1970s until
he retired in 2020, and founder of In Touch Ministries (1977), whose radio and
television broadcasts carried his teaching worldwide. He wrote more than forty
books.

## What he is known for teaching

- Trusting God in adversity. In *How to Handle Adversity* (1989) he argued
  that the more important question in suffering is not "why?" but "how should
  I respond?"
- Obeying God and leaving the outcome to him.
- Prayer, and a daily walk with God through his Word.

## On the struggles armor meets

- **Fear and hard seasons:** God is sovereign over what you face, and
  adversity can draw a man closer to God instead of driving him away.
