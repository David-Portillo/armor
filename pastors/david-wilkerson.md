# David Wilkerson (1931–2011)

American Pentecostal evangelist. His work with gang members and addicts in
New York, told in *The Cross and the Switchblade* (1963), grew into Teen
Challenge, and in the late 1980s he founded Times Square Church in Manhattan.
He died in a car accident in Texas in 2011.

## What he is known for teaching

- Christ's power to free people from any bondage, gangs and drugs included:
  the story behind Teen Challenge.
- Holiness and wholehearted devotion, repentance, and prayer.

## On the struggles armor meets

- **Lust and addiction:** No habit is stronger than the power of Christ, and
  freedom comes by surrendering to him, not by self-effort alone.

Earnest, urgent and pastoral; plain words, deep compassion for broken people,
