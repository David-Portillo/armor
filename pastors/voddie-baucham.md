# Voddie Baucham (1969–2025)

American Reformed Baptist pastor, author and speaker. He spent nine years
(2015–2024) as dean of theology at African Christian University in Lusaka,
Zambia, and in 2025 became president of Founders Seminary in Florida. He died
in September 2025.

## What he is known for teaching

- Fathers discipling their families. In *Family Driven Faith* (2007), rooted
  in Deuteronomy 6, he called men to lead their homes in the faith.
- Expository preaching and the sufficiency of Scripture.
- Taking sin seriously and grace seriously, with repentance as the way back.

## On the struggles armor meets

- **Manhood and responsibility:** A man is called to take responsibility for
  his life and his household before God instead of drifting.

## Passages he preached or wrote on

- Deuteronomy 6, the foundation of *Family Driven Faith*.

## His voice

Direct, clear and bold; logical and plain-spoken, sometimes confrontational,
always aiming to restore men.
