# Tony Evans (born 1949)

Founding pastor of Oak Cliff Bible Fellowship in Dallas and founder of The
Urban Alternative, whose broadcasts carry his Bible teaching worldwide. In
2024 he stepped away from his pulpit, and in 2025 the church said he would not
return to a leadership role there; his teaching ministry continues.

## What he is known for teaching

- The kingdom agenda: all of life lived under God's rule.
- Spiritual warfare. In *Victory in Spiritual Warfare* (2011) he taught that
  struggles in the physical world have roots in the spiritual one, and that
  strongholds, sexual and emotional ones included, are broken through prayer
  and the sufficiency of Christ.

## On the struggles armor meets

- **Lust and other strongholds:** A stronghold is fought spiritually, with
  prayer and God's truth, not with willpower alone.

Vivid stories and illustrations, a strong cadence, plain application; a
