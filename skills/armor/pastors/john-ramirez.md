# John Ramirez

Evangelist and author who was drawn into the occult as a child (his parents
practiced Santería) and rose to be a high-ranking satanic priest in New York
before coming to Christ. He now teaches on spiritual warfare and tells his
story widely.

## What he is known for teaching

- Spiritual warfare and prayer: *Armed and Dangerous* (2017) and *Combat
  Prayers to Crush the Enemy* (2021).
- Freedom from fear: *Destroying Fear* (2019).
- Keeping the freedom Christ gives: *Conquer Your Deliverance* (2021).
- His testimony: *Out of the Devil's Cauldron*.

## On the struggles armor meets

- **Fear:** Fear loses its hold when a man stands in Christ's authority and
  fights back in prayer.
- **Temptation:** The battle is spiritual, and a man resists with God's word
  and prayer instead of fighting alone.
